| **Muang Thai Capital** | Market rate | 36-60 months | Motorcycle specialist |

### Tips for Getting a Loan

1. **Shop around** - Bank rates are usually lower than dealer finance
2. **Required documents**: ID card, income proof, work permit (if foreigner)
3. **Loan terms**: Typically 36-60 months
4. **Collateral**: The scooter itself secures the loan
5. **Foreigners**: Need 1+ year work permit and salary proof

### Interest Rate Comparison

| Source | Typical Rate |
|--------|--------------|
| **Bank loan (new EV)** | 4-6% p.a. |
| **Dealer finance** | 16-23% p.a. |
| **Savings** | 0% (pay cash!) |

**Recommendation**: Get pre-approved from a bank before visiting the dealer. Dealer finance rates can be 16-23% - banks offer much better rates around 4-6%.
