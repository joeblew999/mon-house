## 8.1 Double Bed

**Product Information**
- **Type**: Double Bed / Queen Size Bed (เตียงนอนคู่)
- **Style**: Modern/minimalist
- **Size**: Queen (152 × 198 cm) or King (183 × 198 cm)

**Requirements**
- Sturdy frame construction
- Headboard preferred
- Under-bed storage optional
- Easy assembly
- Neutral colors (white, grey, wood tones)

**Standard Dimensions**

| Size | Width | Length | Footprint |
|------|-------|--------|-----------|
| Queen | 152 cm | 198 cm | 152 × 198 cm |
| King | 183 cm | 198 cm | 183 × 198 cm |

**Note**: Add 10-20 cm each side for frame/headboard overhang.

**Placement in Floor Plan**
- **Drawing**: proposed_01/plan.svg
- **Room**: Each bedroom (1 bed per bedroom)
- **Position**: Corner away from door, headboard against wall
- **Orientation**: Place in corner furthest from bedroom door
- **Clearance**: Minimum 60 cm walking space on accessible sides

**Mattress (separate)**
- Standard Thai mattress sizes match bed frames
- Consider firmness preference
- Budget separately from bed frame

## 8.2 Bedroom Doors (Both Bedrooms)

**Product Information**
- **Type**: Interior swing door (ประตูห้องนอน)
- **Quantity**: 2 doors (one per bedroom)
- **Purpose**: Replace existing bedroom doors

**Standard Thailand Door Sizes**

| Size | Width | Height | Notes |
|------|-------|--------|-------|
| Standard | 80 cm | 200 cm | Most common |
| Wide | 90 cm | 200 cm | Common alternative |

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT**: Measure existing door frames before purchasing. Sizes above are standard Thailand dimensions - actual frame size may vary.

**Requirements**
- Match existing door frame dimensions
- Interior grade (not exterior/weatherproof)
- Swing direction: Check existing hinges
- Color: TBD (white, wood grain, or match house style)
- Handle/lock: Privacy lock for bedrooms

**Door Types Available**

| Type | Description | Price Range |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Hollow core | Lightweight, budget option | ฿800-1,500 |
| Solid core | Heavier, better sound insulation | ฿1,500-3,000 |
| Wood veneer | Real wood surface, mid-range | ฿2,000-4,000 |
| Solid wood | Premium, best quality | ฿4,000-10,000+ |

**Before Purchasing - Measure:**
- [ ] Door width (inside frame)
- [ ] Door height (inside frame)
- [ ] Frame thickness
- [ ] Hinge side (left or right)
- [ ] Swing direction (inward or outward)

**Where to Buy**
- **HomePro**: Wide selection of interior doors
- **Thai Watsadu**: Budget-friendly options
- **Boonthavorn**: Quality doors and hardware

**Installation**
- If same size as existing: Easy replacement
- If different size: May need frame modification
- Standard installation labor: ฿500-1,000 per door

## 8.3 Wall Shelves (Bedroom 2 Only)

**Product Information**
- **Type**: Floating wall shelf (ชั้นวางผนัง)
- **Location**: Bedroom 2 only
- **Source**: IKEA Thailand
- **Purpose**: Display and storage

**Requirements**
- Simple floating design
- Hidden mounting brackets preferred
- Neutral colors (white, wood tones)
- Suitable wall mounting for Thai construction

**Recommended: IKEA LACK Wall Shelf**

| Size | Dimensions | Price | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|-------|
| Small | 30 × 26 cm | ฿249 | Compact, accent display |
| Medium | 110 × 26 cm | ฿690 | **Recommended** |
| Large | 190 × 26 cm | ฿790 | Long wall coverage |

**Features**
- Hidden suspension brackets (invisible once installed)
- Colors: White, White stained oak effect, Black-brown
- Easy to install
- 26 cm depth suitable for books, plants, decor

**Installation Notes**
- Requires wall anchors for Thai concrete/brick walls
- Use appropriate fixings (not included with shelf)
- Check wall construction before drilling
