## Installation Requirements

### Wall Mounting in Thailand

**Thai walls are typically:**
- Concrete block
- Brick with plaster
- Some gypsum/drywall (newer construction)

**Required tools and supplies:**
- Drill with masonry bit (for concrete/brick)
- Wall plugs/anchors (plastic or metal)
- Level
- Pencil for marking

**LACK shelf installation:**
1. Mark hole positions using included template
2. Drill holes with masonry bit
3. Insert wall plugs
4. Attach bracket to wall
5. Slide shelf onto hidden bracket
