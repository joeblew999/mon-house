## 5.4 External Power Outlets

**See**: [Electrics - Outdoor Power Outlets](../electrics/SPEC.md#46-outdoor-power-outlets) for full specifications.

**Summary**
- **Type**: Weatherproof outdoor power outlets (เต้าเสียบไฟฟ้ากันน้ำ)
- **Purpose**: Charging electric scooter, outdoor power tools, garden equipment
- **Rating**: IP44 or higher, 220V/16A minimum
- **Quantity**: 3-4 outlets recommended

**Locations**
- **Carport**: 1-2 outlets for scooter charging
- **Garden**: 1 outlet for tools
- **Patio**: 1 outlet for outdoor use
