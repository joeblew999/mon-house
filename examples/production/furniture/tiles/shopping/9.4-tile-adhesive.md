## Usage Notes

### Mixing
- Add powder to water (not water to powder)
- Mix until smooth paste, no lumps
- Let rest 5 minutes, mix again
- Use within 2-3 hours

### Application
- Apply with notched trowel
- 6-8mm notch for 60×60 tiles
- Back-butter large tiles for better coverage
- Press tile firmly, twist slightly

### Tips
- Work in small sections
- Clean excess before it dries
- Keep bags dry until use
