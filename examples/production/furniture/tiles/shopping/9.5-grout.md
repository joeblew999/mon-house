## Product Details

### Grout Requirements
- **Colour**: Match Marbella Desa-Cream tile colour
- **Joint width**: 2 mm (narrow for modern look)
- **Type**: Cementitious grout (standard)
- **Coverage**: ~0.1-0.15 kg per m² for 2mm joints

### Why Narrow 2mm Joints?
- Modern, seamless appearance
- Less grout visible = cleaner look
- Large 60×60 tiles look best with narrow gaps
- Matches contemporary design

## Colour Matching Tips

### For Desa-Cream Tiles:
- Look for: Cream, Beige, Ivory, Off-white
- Avoid: Pure white (too much contrast)
- Avoid: Dark colours (visible joints)
- **Best approach**: Bring actual tile to store

### Alternative: Contrasting Grout
If you want grout lines visible:
- Light grey grout creates subtle definition
- Can highlight tile pattern
- Personal preference

---

## Application Notes

### Mixing
- Add powder to water slowly
- Mix to thick paste consistency
- Let rest 5 minutes before use
- Use within 1-2 hours

### Application
- Wait 24 hours after tile adhesive cures
- Apply with rubber float at 45° angle
- Push grout into joints
- Clean excess with damp sponge
- Final clean after 15-20 minutes

### Curing
- Avoid walking on for 24 hours
- Keep dry for 48-72 hours
- Seal bathroom grout after 7 days (optional)
