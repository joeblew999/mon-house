## Tips for Choosing

1. **Match the style** - Look for sleek, low-profile designs like Molesun
2. **Check material** - Tech leather/PU leather is easy to clean in Thailand's climate
3. **Read reviews** - Look for durability feedback
4. **Check dimensions** - Measure your space first
5. **Consider delivery** - Large items may have extra shipping costs
