## Requirements
- Brand: Panasonic Wide Series (WEGN)
- Color: Black (matches switches)
- Weatherproof rating: IP44 or higher for outdoor
- Voltage: 220V AC (Thailand standard)
- Rating: Minimum 16A

## Installation Notes

### Indoor
- Use same black metallic face plates as switches (WEG680xMB)
- Consistent modern look throughout house

### Outdoor
- ⚡ **RCD/GFCI Protection**: All outdoor outlets MUST be on RCD-protected circuit
- **Height**: Install 30-50cm above ground level
- **Cover orientation**: Flip cover should open downward
- **Coordinate**: Discuss exact locations with electrician during rough-in
