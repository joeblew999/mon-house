## Installation Notes

### Indoor
- Match outlet plates with switch plates for consistent look
- Use GFCI/RCD protection for bathroom outlet

### Outdoor
- ⚡ **RCD/GFCI Protection**: All outdoor outlets MUST be on RCD-protected circuit
- **Height**: Install 30-50cm above ground level
- **Cover orientation**: Flip cover should open downward
- **Coordinate**: Discuss exact locations with electrician during rough-in
