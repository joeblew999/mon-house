## Installation Notes

**Wall Lights:**
- Mount at eye level or slightly higher (~1.8-2.0m)
- Connect to outdoor lighting circuit
- Coordinate with electrician during rough-in
- Use weatherproof junction box

**Garden Post Lights:**
- Install on concrete base or ground spike
- Run underground cable in conduit
- Consider solar alternative for easier install

**Solar Lights:**
- No wiring needed - easiest option
- Place where direct sunlight hits solar panel
- Good for garden accent, not primary lighting

---

## Builder Notes

- ⚡ Coordinate outdoor lighting with electrician during rough-in
- Run separate circuit for outdoor lights
- Use RCD/GFCI protection for outdoor circuits
- Consider timer or motion sensor for entry lights
