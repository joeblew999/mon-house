## Installation Tips

1. **Height**: Center of screen at seated eye level when on sofa
2. **Distance from projector**: Match projector throw ratio (2-3m for XGIMI MoGo 4)
3. **Power outlet**: Need outlet nearby for motorized screen
4. **Wall strength**: Use appropriate wall anchors
5. **Level**: Use a spirit level during installation
