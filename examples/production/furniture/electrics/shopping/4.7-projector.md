## AirPlay Note

**Important**: Due to Netflix DRM restrictions, you cannot AirPlay Netflix content from iPhone/iPad to any projector - the screen will be black.

**Workarounds:**
- Use the projector's built-in Netflix app (recommended)
- Connect via HDMI cable from laptop
- AirPlay works for other content (YouTube, photos, videos)

For full Netflix + AirPlay functionality, use:
- Built-in Netflix app for Netflix content
- AirPlay for non-DRM content (photos, YouTube, etc.)
