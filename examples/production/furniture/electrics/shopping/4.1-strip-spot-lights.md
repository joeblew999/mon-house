## Dimming Options Explained

| Option | How It Works | Pros | Cons |
|--------|--------------|------|------|
| **Built-in Dimmable Track Light** | Dimmer control on the fixture itself | No switch replacement needed, simple | Must buy specific dimmable models |
| **Dimmer Switch + Regular Light** | Replace wall switch with dimmer | Works with any dimmable LED | Requires electrical work |
| **Smart Dimmer Switch** | WiFi dimmer, app/voice control | Most flexible, smart home ready | More expensive, needs WiFi |
| **GU10 with Dimmable Bulb** | Use dimmable GU10 bulb + dimmer switch | Replaceable bulbs | Need both dimmable bulb AND dimmer switch |

**Recommendation for "no screw around with switches":**
- **Best option**: Buy track lights with **built-in dimming** (items #5, #6, #7)
- These have a dimmer knob/control on the fixture - no need to change any wall switches!

**If you already have non-dimmable lights:**
- Replace wall switch with Dyno LED Dimmer (#8) - Thai brand, reliable
- Or use Tuya Smart Dimmer (#10) for app/voice control

## Bulb Type Comparison

| Bulb Type | Pros | Cons |
|-----------|------|------|
| **GU10** | Easy bulb replacement, standard size, many options | Less common in track lights, slightly more expensive |
| **E27** | Very common, cheapest bulbs | Larger fixture needed, fewer track light options |
| **Built-in LED** | Brightest, most efficient, slim design | Cannot replace bulb, whole fixture must be replaced |

## Notes

- GU10 bulbs are preferred for easy replacement but harder to find in track lights
- Most track lights in Thailand use built-in LED (integrated)
- Make sure track rail and spot lights are compatible (2-wire system is most common)
- Black fixtures work well with modern/industrial design
- White fixtures blend better with ceilings
- Buy extra track rails for flexibility in positioning

### Transformer/Driver Notes

- **AC 220V track lights**: No transformer needed - plug directly into Thailand's 220V power
- **DC 12V/24V track lights**: Require a transformer/driver (not recommended for simplicity)
- **All products listed above**: Work with AC 220V (Thailand standard) - no transformer needed!

### Dimming Notes

- **Built-in dimmable lights**: Easiest option - no switch changes needed
- **Non-dimmable lights + dimmer switch**: Requires electrician to replace wall switch
- **Important**: Not all LED lights are dimmable. If using dimmer switch, ensure the LED is marked as "dimmable"
