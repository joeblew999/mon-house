## 4.1 Strip Spot Lights (Track Lighting)

**Product Information**
- **Type**: Track/strip mounted spot lights (โคมไฟสปอตไลท์ราง)
- **Style**: Modern/minimalist
- **Purpose**: Mood lighting throughout all rooms
- **Installation**: Attach to suspended ceiling metal strip/track

**Requirements**
- Mount type: Track/strip mount for suspended ceiling
- Color: Black or White
- Bulb type: GU10 preferred, E27 acceptable
- Adjustable: Spot head should be rotatable/adjustable
- Style: Minimalist, modern
- **Dimming**: Preferred - for mood lighting control without changing switches

**Preferred Bulb Types (in order)**
1. **GU10** - Preferred (compact, focused beam, easy replacement)
2. **E27** - Acceptable (common, easy to find bulbs)
3. **Built-in LED** - Last resort (harder to replace)

**Placement**
- **Living Room**: Mood/accent lighting
- **Bedroom**: Ambient lighting
- **Dining Area**: Above table accent
- **All rooms**: General mood lighting

## 4.7 Projector

**Product Information**
- **Type**: Smart projector with streaming (โปรเจคเตอร์อัจฉริยะ)
- **Purpose**: Home entertainment - movies, TV shows, presentations
- **Installation**: Ceiling mount or shelf placement

**Requirements**

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Netflix** | Built-in Netflix app (officially licensed) |
| **AirPlay** | Apple AirPlay 2 support |
| **Resolution** | 1080p minimum, 4K preferred |
| **Brightness** | 800+ ANSI lumens (for daytime viewing) |
| **Smart OS** | Android TV, Google TV, or proprietary with app store |
| **Throw ratio** | Short throw preferred for small room |
| **Audio** | Built-in speakers (or Bluetooth for external) |
| **Connectivity** | WiFi, HDMI, Bluetooth |

**Streaming Requirements**
- **Netflix**: Must have official Netflix certification (not sideloaded)
- **Apple AirPlay**: Native AirPlay 2 for screen mirroring from iPhone/iPad/Mac
- **YouTube**: Built-in app
- **Other**: Disney+, Prime Video (nice to have)

**Placement**
- **Room**: Living room
- **Position**: Ceiling mounted or on TV stand shelf
- **Screen**: Project onto white wall or pull-down screen
- **Distance**: 2-3m from screen (depends on throw ratio)

**Power Requirements**
- Standard 220V outlet near mounting location
- Consider ceiling outlet if ceiling-mounted

## 4.10 Projector Screen

**Product Information**
- **Type**: Projection screen (จอโปรเจคเตอร์)
- **Purpose**: Viewing surface for projector
- **Installation**: Wall-mounted or tripod/portable

**Requirements**

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Size** | 80-100 inches diagonal |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 16:9 (widescreen) |
| **Type** | Pull-down manual or motorized |
| **Material** | Matte white (for wide viewing angle) |
| **Gain** | 1.0-1.2 (standard gain) |

**Screen Types**

### 4.10.1 Pull-Down Manual Screen
- **Pros**: Affordable, no power needed, reliable
- **Cons**: Manual operation, may curl over time
- **Best for**: Budget-conscious, occasional use

### 4.10.2 Motorized Screen
- **Pros**: Convenient, smooth operation, looks premium
- **Cons**: More expensive, requires power outlet
- **Best for**: Frequent use, modern setup

### 4.10.3 Tripod/Portable Screen
- **Pros**: Portable, no installation needed
- **Cons**: Takes floor space, less stable
- **Best for**: Rental or temporary setup

**Placement**
- **Room**: Living room
- **Position**: Wall opposite to projector location
- **Height**: Center of screen at eye level when seated
- **Distance**: Match projector throw distance

**Alternative**: White Wall
- If wall is smooth and painted matte white, can project directly
- Saves money but lower image quality than dedicated screen
