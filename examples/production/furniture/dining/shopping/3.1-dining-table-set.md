## Requirements

- **Table top**: Tempered glass (กระจกนิรภัย)
- **Legs**: Metal (chrome preferred, black steel acceptable)
- **Seating**: 4 chairs
- **Size**: ~110-140 × 70-80 cm (rectangular) or ~100-120 cm (round)
- **Style**: Modern/minimalist
- **Budget**: ฿2,000 - ฿8,000 range
