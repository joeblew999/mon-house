## Product Details

### Undermount Sink Requirements
- **Installation**: Undermount (installed from below benchtop)
- **Material**: Stainless steel (SUS304 preferred)
- **Bowl**: Single bowl (more workspace)
- **Size**: Match benchtop cutout dimensions

### Features Required
- **SUS304 stainless steel** - corrosion resistant
- **Sound dampening** - reduces noise
- **Drain included** - basket strainer type
- **Undermount clips** - for solid surface installation

### Why Undermount?
- Clean, seamless look with solid surface
- Easy to wipe crumbs directly into sink
- No lip to trap dirt
- Modern, minimalist appearance
- Works perfectly with HI-MACS benchtop
