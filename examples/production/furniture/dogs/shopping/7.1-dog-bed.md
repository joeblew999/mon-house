## Size Guide | คำแนะนำขนาด

For 2 dogs sleeping together:
- **Medium dogs (10-20kg each)**: XL bed (100+ cm) should work
- **Large dogs (20-35kg each)**: Consider XXL (120+ cm) or 2 separate beds
- **Giant breeds**: Definitely need 2 separate beds
