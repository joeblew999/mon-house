## Shopping Checklist

When you go to HomePro/Global House:

1. **Ask for**: "COTTO wall-mounted basin, small size, about 55cm wide"
   - Thai: "อ่างล้างหน้าแขวนผนัง COTTO ขนาดเล็ก กว้างประมาณ 55 ซม."

2. **Check**:
   - [ ] Basin has wall mounting brackets included
   - [ ] Basin size fits your false wall space (measure first!)
   - [ ] White ceramic, no cracks or chips
   - [ ] Overflow hole present (prevents flooding)
   - [ ] Check if tap hole pre-drilled (or no hole if using wall-mounted tap)

3. **Buy at same time**:
   - [ ] Basin tap (see 2.6-basin-taps.md for recommendation)
   - [ ] P-trap drain kit (ask staff for basin drain set)
   - [ ] Flexible water supply hoses (if not included with tap)
   - [ ] Silicone sealant (clear or white)

## Maintenance

- **Cleaning**: Wipe with damp cloth daily
- **Avoid**: Abrasive cleaners (scratch ceramic)
- **Use**: Mild bathroom cleaner or dish soap
- **Drain care**: Clean P-trap every 6-12 months (remove hair/debris)

---

## Questions to Ask at Store

1. Does this basin include mounting brackets?
2. What size P-trap/drain do I need for this basin?
3. Do you have a matching tap in stock?
4. What is the return policy if it doesn't fit?
5. Can you recommend a plumber for installation?
6. Is this model currently in stock or do I need to order?
