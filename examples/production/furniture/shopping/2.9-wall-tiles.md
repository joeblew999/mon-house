**How Many Tiles to Buy:**

**Full Height Tiling Calculation** (recommended for small bathroom):
- Measure wall area (perimeter × height)
- Example: Bathroom perimeter 10m × 2.4m ceiling = 24 m²
- Subtract door/window area: ~2 m²
- Net wall area: 22 m²
- Add 15% waste: 22 m² × 1.15 = 25.3 m²
- Tiles needed: 25.3 m² ÷ 0.05 m²/tile = 506 tiles
- Cost: 506 tiles × ฿18 = ฿9,108

**Half Height Tiling** (budget option):
- Tile only 1.2m high in dry areas
- Saves ~40% on tiles
- Still requires full height in shower area

**AVOID**: Dark tiles (makes small bathroom dark and cramped)

---

## Glossy vs Matte

**Glossy (RECOMMENDED for walls):**
- ✓ Reflects light → brighter bathroom
- ✓ Easier to clean (smooth surface)
- ✓ Water beads off (dries faster)
- ✓ Cheaper than matte

**Matte:**
- Modern look
- Hides water spots better
- More expensive
- Less light reflection

**DECISION**: Glossy white - brightest, cheapest, easiest to clean.

## Coverage Strategy

**Option 1: Full Height (Floor to Ceiling) - RECOMMENDED**
- Best for Thailand humidity
- Full waterproof protection
- Easiest to clean (tile entire wall)
- More expensive (~฿9,000 materials for small bathroom)
- Most durable

**Option 2: Half Height + Paint Top**
- Tile 1.2m high, paint above
- Saves money (~฿5,500 materials)
- Must use waterproof paint above tiles
- MUST tile full height in shower zone!

**RECOMMENDATION**: Full height if budget allows - best for humid Thailand climate.

## Installation Notes

**Professional Tiler REQUIRED**: ฿5,000-฿10,000 labor for full-height wall tiling

**Installation Sequence:**
1. **Waterproof membrane** in shower zone (CRITICAL!)
2. **Start tiling from bottom** (use spacers for even grout lines)
3. **Cut tiles** around outlets, switches, pipes
4. **Shower area** gets full waterproofing + full-height tiles
5. **Let set 24 hours** before grouting
6. **Grout application** (waterproof grout in shower area)
7. **Clean** excess grout before it dries
8. **Seal grout** in shower area (prevents mold)
