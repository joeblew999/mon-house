## HomePro Shopping Trip Plan

**Bring**:
- [ ] Phone/camera (fully charged!)
- [ ] This checklist
- [ ] Tape measure
- [ ] Notepad + pen (write down prices, model numbers)
- [ ] Measurements from bathroom

**Route Through HomePro** (efficient path):

1. **Tiles Section** (usually at back/warehouse area)
   - Floor tiles (grey anti-slip)
   - Wall tiles (white glossy)
   - Photo both + price labels

2. **Bathroom Fixtures** (main showroom area)
   - COTTO section: toilet, basin, taps, shower
   - Photo each product + model numbers

3. **Bathroom Accessories** (aisle near fixtures)
   - Towel bars, TP holder, waste bin
   - Try to match finish (chrome)

4. **Glass/Mirror Section** (near doors/windows)
   - Frameless mirror samples
   - Photo price per sqm sign

5. **Lighting Section**
   - LED bathroom lights
   - Photo recommended fixtures

6. **Plumbing Section**
   - Floor drain
   - Ventilation fan may be here or electrical section

7. **Water Heaters** (usually separate area or outdoor section)
   - Tank water heaters
   - Photo recommended model

**At Each Section**:
1. Find product matching our recommendation
2. Take photo showing:
   - Product itself (close-up)
   - Price label/sign
   - Model number if visible
3. Ask staff questions from shopping files
4. Note any differences from expected price

---

## Photo Guidelines

**Good Product Photo Includes**:
- Clear view of product
- Product label/brand visible
- Price visible (if in same shot)
- Good lighting (use phone flash if needed)
- No blur (hold phone steady)

**Take Multiple Shots**:
- Wide shot (product in context)
- Close-up (details, texture, finish)
- Price label (clear, readable)
- Model number/spec label

**Organize Photos**:
1. Transfer to computer same day
2. Rename files using naming convention
3. Place in `shopping/images/` folder
4. Update shopping files with actual prices if different

---

## After Photo Collection

1. Review all photos - are they clear and useful?
2. Rename using proper convention (see README.md)
3. Move to `shopping/images/` folder
4. Update shopping .md files with:
   - Actual product model numbers
   - Current prices (if different from estimate)
   - Any notes about availability
5. Commit to git with photos
