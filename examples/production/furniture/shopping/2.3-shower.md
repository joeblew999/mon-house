## NOTE: This is Not a Separate Purchase

**The "shower" consists of:**
1. **Shower taps/mixer** → See [2.5-shower-taps.md](2.5-shower-taps.md) ✅
2. **Shower glass panel** → See [2.4-shower-glass-panel.md](2.4-shower-glass-panel.md) ✅
3. **Floor tiles** (sloped to drain) → See [2.8-floor-tiles.md](2.8-floor-tiles.md) ✅
4. **Wall tiles** (full height waterproofing) → See [2.9-wall-tiles.md](2.9-wall-tiles.md) ✅
5. **Floor drain** → See [2.17-floor-drain.md](2.17-floor-drain.md) ✅

**The shower is created through:**
- Wet zone floor tiling (with slope to drain)
- Waterproof membrane application
- Full-height wall tiling in shower area
- Glass panel wet/dry separation
- Floor drain installation
- Shower mixer + hand shower installation

**No separate "shower" product to buy!**

---

## Shower Construction Checklist

**Waterproofing (CRITICAL!):**
- [ ] Waterproof membrane on floor
- [ ] Waterproof membrane on shower walls (full height)
- [ ] 24-hour water test before tiling

**Floor Work:**
- [ ] Floor sloped 1-2% toward drain
- [ ] Floor drain installed at lowest point
- [ ] Anti-slip floor tiles (see 2.8-floor-tiles.md)

**Wall Work:**
- [ ] Full-height wall tiles in shower zone
- [ ] Waterproof grout
- [ ] Sealed grout lines (prevents mold)

**Fixtures:**
- [ ] Shower mixer installed (see 2.5-shower-taps.md)
- [ ] Glass panel installed (see 2.4-shower-glass-panel.md)
- [ ] Test water flow and drainage

**Total Shower Construction Cost:**
- Tiles (floor + walls): ~฿10,000
- Waterproofing: ~฿2,000
- Shower taps: ~฿1,500
- Glass panel: ~฿3,000
- Floor drain: ~฿500
- Labor: ~฿8,000-12,000
- **Total**: ~฿25,000-30,000 for complete shower area

## Recommended Shower Dimensions

- **Minimum**: 90cm × 90cm (tight for budget bathroom)
- **Recommended**: 100cm × 100cm or 80cm × 120cm
- **Comfortable**: 120cm × 120cm (if space allows)

**For small bathroom**: Use 80cm-100cm width, glass panel for wet/dry separation
