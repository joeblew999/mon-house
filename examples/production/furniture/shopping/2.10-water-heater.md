## Mounting Location

**CRITICAL**: Must be mounted OUTSIDE bathroom (external):

**Good Locations:**
- Back porch/balcony (covered)
- Utility area outside building
- Under roof overhang (protected from rain)
- External wall with weatherproof cover

**Requirements:**
- Protected from direct rain (needs cover/enclosure)
- Good ventilation
- Near bathroom (shorter hot water pipes = less heat loss)
- Access to 220V electrical
- Strong wall for mounting (tank + water = ~20kg)

**AVOID**:
- Direct sun exposure (shortens tank life)
- Completely exposed to weather (must have cover!)
- Inside small bathroom (takes up space)

## Installation Requirements

**Professional Electrician + Plumber REQUIRED**: ฿2,000-฿4,000 labor

**Electrical Work:**
- Dedicated circuit breaker (30A RCBO/ELCB)
- 6mm² cable from panel to heater
- Weatherproof junction box
- Grounding wire (CRITICAL for safety!)

**Plumbing Work:**
- Cold water inlet pipe to tank
- Hot water outlet pipe to bathroom (mixer taps)
- Pipe insulation (keeps water hot longer)
- Pressure relief valve installation
- Drainage for safety valve overflow

**Critical Safety:**
- MUST have RCBO/ELCB protection (electric shock prevention)
- Proper grounding
- Weatherproof connections
- Safety valve must drain to safe location
