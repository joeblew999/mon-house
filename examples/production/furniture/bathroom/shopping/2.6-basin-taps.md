## Finish Selection Guide

**Chrome (RECOMMENDED):**
- ✓ Cheapest option
- ✓ Classic look
- ✓ Easy to clean
- ✓ Hides water spots better than black
- ✓ Matches most bathroom accessories

**Brushed Nickel:**
- Mid-price (฿1,200-฿1,800)
- Modern look
- Hides fingerprints well
- Good if you want warmer tone

**Matte Black:**
- Most expensive (฿1,500-฿3,000)
- Very modern/minimalist
- Shows water spots easily
- Needs daily wiping

**DECISION**: Go with **Chrome** - cheapest, easiest, most practical.

## Installation Notes

**DIY-friendly** if you have basic tools:
1. Insert tap through basin hole from top
2. Secure from underneath with mounting nut and washer
3. Connect flexible hoses to hot/cold water supplies
4. Use Teflon tape on all threaded connections
5. Turn on water, check for leaks
6. Adjust flow with handle

**OR hire plumber**: ฿500-฿1,000 labor to install basin + tap together

## Maintenance

- **Daily**: Wipe with dry cloth after use
- **Weekly**: Clean with mild soap and soft cloth
- **Avoid**: Abrasive cleaners, steel wool (scratches chrome)
- **Aerator**: Clean every 6 months (removes mineral buildup)
