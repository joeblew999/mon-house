## Installation

**What's Needed:**
- Linear drain body with trap
- Waterproof membrane (installed by tiler)
- Mortar bed (sloped toward drain)

**Installation Notes:**
- Install DURING floor tiling (not after)
- Floor must slope 1-2% toward drain
- Waterproof membrane must seal around drain
- Test for leaks before finishing tiles

**Labor Cost:** Included in floor tiling cost
