## Why Hand Shower (Not Rain Shower)

For cheap minimalist bathroom:
- ✓ Hand shower much cheaper (฿1,500 vs ฿4,000+ for rain shower)
- ✓ More flexible (can direct water where needed)
- ✓ Better for small bathroom (no ceiling mounting needed)
- ✓ Thailand standard (everyone uses hand showers)
- ✓ Water-efficient (less water per minute than rain shower)
- ✓ Easy to clean bathroom with (spray down tiles)

Rain shower = luxury feature, not needed for budget project.

## Installation Notes

**Professional plumber RECOMMENDED** (฿1,000-฿1,500 labor):
- Need to install during wall tiling phase
- Hot + cold water pipes must be rough-in correctly
- Mixer height: ~1.0-1.2m from floor
- Shower holder height: ~1.8-2.0m from floor
- Ensure proper pipe sealing (prevent leaks inside wall)

**Critical**: Install BEFORE wall tiles finished! Pipes must be rough-in during construction.

## Maintenance

- **Daily**: Wipe mixer and shower head dry (prevent water spots)
- **Weekly**: Clean shower head spray holes with soft brush
- **Monthly**: Soak shower head in vinegar water (remove mineral buildup)
- **Yearly**: Check hose for leaks, replace if cracked (hose ~฿200)
