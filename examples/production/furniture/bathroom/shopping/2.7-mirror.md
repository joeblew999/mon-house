## Installation Guide

**Materials Needed:**
- Mirror (cut to size)
- Mirror adhesive (฿150) - specific for mirrors, NOT regular glue
- 4-6 mirror clips (฿50-100)
- Level tool
- Pencil
- Clean cloth

**Steps:**
1. **Clean wall surface** - must be smooth, clean, dry
2. **Mark mirror position** - use level, mark corners with pencil
3. **Install bottom clips** - screw clips at bottom edge (support mirror)
4. **Apply adhesive** - dots of mirror adhesive on back of mirror (not full coverage!)
5. **Position mirror** - carefully place on bottom clips, press against wall
6. **Install top clips** - add clips at top to hold while adhesive cures
7. **Let cure** - wait 24 hours before removing temporary clips
8. **Clean** - wipe mirror with glass cleaner

**Professional Installation** (recommended): ฿500-฿1,000 labor
- Mirrors are heavy and fragile
- Professional ensures level mounting
- Prevents cracking during installation
