## Anti-Slip Rating IMPORTANT!

**Check tile label for slip rating:**
- R9: Minimal slip resistance (NOT for bathrooms!)
- **R10**: Good for bathrooms (MINIMUM requirement)
- **R11**: Better for wet areas (RECOMMENDED)
- R12+: Industrial (overkill for home)

**Test at store**: Run wet hand on tile surface - should feel textured, not smooth.

## Installation Notes

**Professional Tiler REQUIRED**: ฿3,000-฿6,000 labor for small bathroom
- Floor must be level before tiling
- Waterproof membrane ESSENTIAL (prevents leaks)
- Floor must slope 1-2% toward drain
- Tiles laid on adhesive bed
- Grout applied after tiles set (24 hours)

**Critical Waterproofing Steps:**
1. Apply waterproof membrane to floor
2. Test membrane (water test 24 hours)
3. Install drain (see 2.17-floor-drain.md)
4. Slope floor toward drain with mortar bed
5. Tile on top of sloped surface
6. Grout with waterproof grout
7. Seal grout (optional but recommended)
