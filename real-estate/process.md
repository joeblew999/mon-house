## How

1. Non-Exclusive Agent Listings

Tell each agent:

"I'm listing with multiple agents, non-exclusive"
"First one to bring a buyer at my price gets the commission"
Standard commission is 3% (negotiate down to 2% if you can)

Key point: Get it in writing that it's non-exclusive - some agents will try to sneak in exclusivity clauses.

2. Facebook Marketplace / Groups


Saves the 3% commission if she finds a buyer directly.
