## Steps

1. **Prep** - Brush off loose dust/chalk with a stiff broom. No pressure washing needed.
2. **Prime walls + fence** - TOA Contact Primer, 1 coat. Bonds to chalky old plaster.
3. **Topcoat walls + fence** - TOA SuperShield Sheen, 2 coats. Self-cleaning titanium tech.
4. **Prime timber** - TOA Undercoat G1600, 1-2 coats. Blocks tannin bleed.
5. **Topcoat timber** - TOA TimberShield, 2 coats. Flexible acrylic for wood.

---

## Notes

- House walls are dusty/chalky - Contact Primer is designed for this.
- Must be **Sheen** finish on walls (hides old plaster imperfections).
- 14 cans total from HomePro (5 primer + 8 topcoat + 1 wood primer + 1 wood topcoat).
