DIY work, Joe + Mon. **Not part of any builder quote.** Replace the existing flat garage roof sheeting and repaint the supporting steelwork. Gloss black throughout, including the sheet undersides.

## Scope

- **Total area:** 25 m² (Roof 1: 3 × 3 = 9 m²; Roof 2: 4 × 4 = 16 m²).
- **Existing beams:** 50 × 50 mm at ~1 × 1 m spacing. Reused as-is, just repainted.
- **Fascia:** unweld, reposition outward and higher so sheets sit hidden behind it, re-weld, grind smooth. No replacement.
- **Slope:** ~5° minimum for drainage.

Out of scope: structural rust repair, gates (see [GATE-01](GATE-01.md) / [GATE-02](GATE-02.md)).

---

## Materials

- **Sheet:** plain ribbed/corrugated metal, paintable (image above). Underside painted gloss black on the ground before fixing — single paint product across the whole job.
- **Fixings:** Tek screws 8 × 1" + rubber washers, ~8 / m² → ~200 screws (1 pack of 250).
- **Paint:** `metal-2in1` — full product details inlined below from the shared catalog partial. See [PAINT.md](PAINT.md) for the formula and project-wide rollup.
- **Paint area:** 25 m² (sheet undersides) + ~5 m² (beams: 50×50 mm × 4 sides × ~25 lin m) = **~30 m²**.
- **Cans:** `ceil(30 ÷ 3.5)` = **9 cans** (effective coverage 3.5 m²/can). Buy 10 to allow for wastage. Subtotal ~2,000 THB.

## Order of work

1. **Structural prep** — fascia reposition, weld, grind.
2. **Paint sheet undersides on the ground** — wire-brush, 2 coats, recoat after 1–2 hrs.
3. **Fix sheets** to beams.
4. **Paint beams** — wire-brush surface rust, 2 coats. *After* all welding (welding burns paint).

---

## Open items

- Fascia-plate offset (outward distance + height) — confirm on site before unwelding.
- Beam rust depth — wire-brush prep covers surface rust only; structural rust is a separate scope.
- **Source + price the plain paintable sheet locally.** Confirm gauge (≥ 0.3 mm) and profile compatible with 1 × 1 m beam spacing.
- Insulation: plain sheet has no PU foam, so summer heat is a separate decision. Foil or fibreglass blanket between beams is the usual fix. Deferred.
