## Critical design notes

- **Entrance:** basin sits **100 mm clear** of the entry wall on the left. With the 700 mm inward-swinging door on the opposite wall, the **COTTO C013 (420 mm deep)** leaves ~**170 mm minimum clearance** between the door arc and the basin face during swing. Door fully opens — basin doesn't block it. Don't try to stand at the basin while the door is being opened.
- **Glass:** **700 mm wide screen** gives a **680 mm walk-in entrance** — shortened from the original 900 mm to free wall length opposite the toilet for the cabinet. **No support bars or visible profiles** — the aluminium U-channel is chased into the wall and floor, sitting flush, **before the tiles go on**, so the tiles cover the channel completely. Glass appears to grow straight out of the wall and floor.
- **Floor-mounted toilet (~700 mm deep):** wall-hung is too expensive, so the toilet is a standard close-coupled at ~695 mm projection. Walkway in front of the toilet at floor level is **685 mm** clear (1380 − 695). The cabinet on the opposite wall is mounted at 1100 mm AFL and above (see Cabinet bullet) so the floor-level corridor stays unobstructed; corridor narrows to 535 mm only at torso height.
- **Cabinet — MOYA TK-CAB-007** wall-hung wood cabinet with built-in chrome towel rail at the bottom. HomePro SKU 1200274 — [homepro.co.th/p/1200274](https://www.homepro.co.th/p/1200274). **350 W × 150 D × 750 H mm**, slow-close door, 3 adjustable shelves, rubber wood frame + MDF door, bathroom-rated. ฿2,197 THB. ![MOYA TK-CAB-007 cabinet](resources/images/bathroom-proposed-part-cabinet.jpg) Mounted on the right wall **paired with the toilet** (cabinet body y≈735–1085, centred on the toilet's y-range). **Bottom edge at 1100 mm AFL** so the built-in towel rail sits at standard towel-grab height. Top edge at 1850 mm AFL. **Doubles as basin storage + shower storage + towel rail** — no separate shower shelf, no separate towel rail, no separate basin shelf. Floor corridor at the toilet stays at 685 mm; corridor narrows to 535 mm only at 1100–1850 AFL (torso height when standing). Walk-in entry to the shower stays unobstructed (cabinet ends at y≈1085, 165 mm clear of the shower glass line at y=1250). **DIY by Joe + Mon** — builder leaves the wall paint-ready. **Mounting on masonry:** the cabinet ships with generic screws but **masonry anchors are not included**. Add a pack of **DEXZON HNA-8 nylon brick anchors** ([homepro.co.th/p/1176153](https://www.homepro.co.th/p/1176153)) — ~฿29 — use 4 for the cabinet, the rest are spare for other DIY items. ![DEXZON HNA-8 nylon brick anchor](resources/images/bathroom-proposed-part-anchor.jpg)
- **Mirror:** single fixed mirror on the left wall above the basin and toilet, **1250 mm long**, running from the shower glass line up to the entry wall. Frameless, butting cleanly at both vertical ends. Top edge 2100 mm AFL, bottom edge 1000 mm AFL (basin rim sits just above, hides the joint).
- **Existing door stays — no change at all.** No re-hang, no swing change, no new lock, no new frame, no new louvre.
- **Bidet sprayer ("bum gun"):** mount on the left wall in the **150 mm gap between the toilet and the shower glass**. **Tank-mounted clip bracket — no drilling into tiles.** T-valve off the toilet's cold-water inlet so the hose runs hidden behind the cistern.
- **Toilet roll holder:** pick one — **(A)** over-the-glass hook on the shower screen (no drilling, cleanest look), or **(B)** mounted on the side of the basin vanity (basin is only 100 mm from the toilet — natural reach, keeps the long wall clear of fixings).
- **Towel rail:** the **MOYA TK-CAB-007 cabinet has a built-in chrome towel rail** at its bottom edge (mounted at 1100 mm AFL). Grab the towel as you step out of the shower toward the door — the cabinet sits right where you walk past. **No separate towel rail** needed.

## Tiling scope

Less tile than BATHROOM.md — the long wall is **not** tiled full height in this layout.

**Walls that get tiled** (full height to 2100 mm AFL):

1. The wall **behind the shower** — the 1380 mm back wall.
2. The wall **on the left of the shower** — only the 700 mm section that's inside the wet zone.
3. The wall **on the right of the shower** — only the 700 mm section that's inside the wet zone.

That's the "shower box" — three sides full height. The fourth side (front) is the glass screen + walk-in opening (no tile, no wall).

**Plus a small tile splashback** (~500 × 600 mm) behind the basin to stop water running down the paint.

**Floor:** tile the whole bathroom — anti-slip variant in the wet zone, regular variant in the dry zone. Waterproof coating under the wet-zone tiles.

**Painted (NOT tiled):**
- Left wall in the dry zone (above and around basin + toilet — except for the basin splashback above)
- Right wall (door + cabinet area)
- Entry wall (door wall)
- Anything above 2100 mm AFL

**Tile look — modern beige, rectified, tight grout.** Single tile range across walls + floor (anti-slip variant for wet zone). **Hard machined edges**, **small (~2 mm) grout lines**, **beige grout matching the tile**, no edge profiles.

## Defers to BATHROOM.md

These items aren't repeated here — assume the same as [BATHROOM.md](BATHROOM.md) unless the builder asks otherwise:

- **Plumbing tidy** — chase supply / waste runs into the masonry; exposed bottle trap under the basin for cleaning access (the LUZERN trap above is the one).
- **Waterproofing** — liquid-applied coating under the wet-zone floor only. **No sheet membrane.**
- **Ventilation** — passive: AC overpressure → existing door louvre → bathroom window kept open. **No extract fan.**
- **Electrical** — **don't move anything.** Light switch stays in its current position **outside** the bathroom (no rewiring). **No new IP-rated outlet inside** — Mon plugs the hair dryer into a living-room socket. Builder still verifies that the bathroom lighting circuit at the consumer unit is RCD/GFCI-protected; if not, add one — but no other electrical work in scope.
- **Water-heater isolator** — stays on the outside wall as today, not relocated.
