## Tiles — catalog

Canonical catalog of every tile that has come up across the renovation. Each spec picks one from here and either includes this partial or inlines its choice. **No tile entry lives in `bathroom-parts.md`** — that partial covers fixtures only.

Anti-slip is non-negotiable on any wet-zone floor: **R10 minimum, R11 preferred**. Glossy porcelain on a wet bathroom floor is a slip hazard regardless of brand or price.

---

### HomePro picks (cheap + bathroom-safe)

**HomePro house-brand anti-slip 30×30 light grey**

- HomePro tiles section — every branch carries the budget anti-slip line. **No fixed SKU** — pick at the showroom on the day.
- 300 × 300 mm ceramic, **R10 minimum, R11 preferred** anti-slip matte. Verify the rating on the box label; if no rating is shown, pick a different tile.
- ~฿25 / tile, ~฿280 / m²
- Light grey or cream — pick the lightest in-stock anti-slip variant. Light tone makes a small bathroom feel larger.
- 30×30 means more grout lines (visually busier) but more cuts forgiven (good in a small bathroom).
- **Recommended for BATHROOM_BASIC** as the cheapest safe option.

**COTTO Pemo Beige 30×60 — rectified, R11 anti-slip**

![COTTO Pemo Beige tile](resources/images/bathroom-proposed-part-tile.jpg)

- HomePro SKU 1305197 — [homepro.co.th/p/1305197](https://www.homepro.co.th/p/1305197)
- 300 × 600 × 8 mm rectified glazed porcelain, textured surface (ผิวหยาบ)
- **R11 anti-slip rated** (ค่ากันลื่น R11) — wet zone OK as-is, no separate anti-slip variant needed
- 8 tiles per box, 1.44 m² per box → **฿599 / box ≈ ~฿75 / tile, ~฿416 / m²**
- HomePro page lists ห้องน้ำ (bathroom), ห้องครัว (kitchen) and outdoor as suitable uses
- Glazed porcelain, water absorption group BIb
- Cross-spec choice: **BATHROOM-COMPACT** and **BATHROOM_ENLARGE** both use this today.

- ~4–5 m² coverage per bag at 8 mm tile
- ฿199 / bag

**Grout — waterproof grey (any major brand)**

- HomePro / Global House
- Joint width: 2 mm with rectified tile; 3–5 mm with non-rectified
- ~6–8 kg per ~50 m² floor
- ~฿150–300 / bag
- Grey > white in any wet bathroom: hides dirt and mould; preferred regardless of which tile is chosen
