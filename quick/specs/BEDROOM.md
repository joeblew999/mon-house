# Bedroom Combine - Laem Chabang

Phase 02 work. Convert the two existing bedrooms into one larger bedroom and open a single 2 m doorway from that bedroom into the living room.

The new bed is **2 m × 2 m**, with the **head against the exterior wall** and the body centred exactly on the line of the 2 m opening — so the bed and the opening are on the same axis. The relocated air-conditioning indoor unit sits **centred above the bed**, on the exterior wall, pushing air the length of the bed and straight through the 2 m opening into the rest of the house.

This spec is **Phase 02 only** — see [PHASES](AAA-PHASES.md).

**Quote rule:** this file is **one quote line item**. The builder gives a single number for the **BEDROOM COMBINE TOTAL** — not bundled with any other spec, not split across files. 1:1 mapping between this spec and the quote.

## Phase 02 — what gets done

### Demolition

1. **Wall between the two bedrooms** (the **"leaf wall"** — referred to by that name elsewhere in this spec). Full wall removed. The two bedrooms become one.
2. **Both bedroom doors + the short wall stub between them.** Removed together. They form a single **2 m wide opening** between the new combined bedroom and the living room.

That is all the demolition. The wall between the bedrooms and the living room is **not** fully removed — only the door-and-stub section described above. The rest of that wall stays.

### Steel lintel over the 2 m opening

- A **small steel lintel** sits over the new 2 m opening. It carries the load over the opening only — it is **not** a long beam spanning the whole room.
- The wall above the opening is **100 mm thick single-brick masonry** and **is load-bearing**.
- The lintel is inserted **~1 m below the ceiling**, leaving ~1 m of brickwork above it. That brickwork acts as a bridge over the opening — under the **45° load-spread principle**, only the triangular load above the opening transfers to the lintel; the rest of the wall load disperses into the bearings on either side. This is what allows a small lintel section to do the job.
- **Boxing-in will be minimal** because the wall is only 100 mm thick — the boxing just clads the visible faces of the lintel within the line of the existing wall. Bedroom-side back face still must be left accessible for the future curtain track.
- Lintel profile, bearing detail, and end-supports to be confirmed by the builder's structural calculation. <!-- TBC: profile, length, bearing pads. -->

### Ceiling infill where the leaf wall used to be

- When the leaf wall is removed, the ceiling has a strip along that wall's old line that needs to be infilled.
- Patch and finish so the ceiling reads as one continuous surface across the new bedroom.
- This is the **only** ceiling work in scope. The two existing **ceiling-light fittings** stay where they are; do not move them, do not add new ones.

### Move the existing air-conditioning indoor unit (approx 2 m)

- The existing AC indoor head currently sits in one of the bedrooms.
- Relocate it **approximately 2 m** along the exterior wall, so it is mounted **centred above the new 2 m × 2 m bed** (at the head, on the exterior wall) in the new combined bedroom.
- This positions the AC to push air the length of the bedroom and out through the new 2 m opening into the living room — cooling both spaces from a single unit.
- Re-route refrigerant lines, condensate drain, and power as needed for the new position.
- Existing outdoor unit stays where it is. The relocation is along the same exterior wall (the AC head is already mounted on it), so the existing line-set is likely long enough — confirm on site before cutting and re-running.

### Electrical — what stays, what's added

**Stays in place — left alone, still works:**

- The **2 ceiling lights** stay in their current positions.
- The **2 existing power outlets** on the inside of the old bedroom doors stay where they are. After demolition they end up on the wall stubs flanking the new 2 m opening — useful as living-room-side outlets.
- The **2 existing light switches** next to the old doors stay where they are and **still work** — they continue to control the same 2 ceiling lights as before.

**Salvaged from the leaf wall:**

- The power outlet currently on the leaf wall goes when the leaf wall is demolished, but its circuit (cable feed) is **salvaged** and re-routed to feed the new bed-side outlets. **Do not pull the cable back to the consumer unit** — capture the existing feed, then re-route it to the exterior wall.

**New on the exterior wall, flanking the head of the bed:**

- 1 new **bedside light switch** on each side of the bed, wired in **2-way switching** with the corresponding existing door-side switch — so each ceiling light can be turned on or off from **either** the old door-side switch **or** the new bedside switch on the same side. New 2-way wiring runs between each pair.
- 1 new **power outlet** on each side of the bed, mounted on the exterior wall just outside the bed's footprint, at bedside-table height. Fed from the salvaged leaf-wall circuit.

**Net result:**

- Each side of the bed gets 1 new bedside switch + 1 new power outlet, side by side at bedside-table height.
- Each old door position keeps its existing switch + outlet, still working.
- Both switches in each 2-way pair control the same ceiling light.

### Floor patching

**Floor levels match.** The two old bedrooms and the living room all sit at the same floor level — no step at the leaf-wall line, no step at the new 2 m opening. Patches are flat-to-flat.

- **Leaf-wall strip** (where the wall between the two bedrooms stood): patch using the **tiles currently acting as the skirting board on that leaf wall**. Lift them and lay them flat into the floor footprint. Perfect reuse — the tiles match the rest of the floor and the count should be right for the strip length.
- **Door-and-stub strip** (where the doors and the small wall stub between them were): patch the same way, using the skirting tiles from the demolished section. <!-- TBC: confirm there are enough lifted tiles to cover both strips, and that grout colour matches the surrounding floor. -->

See [PHASES](AAA-PHASES.md) Phase 02 for the original idea.

### Curtain over the 2 m opening — later, separate scope

A curtain will be added later to close the 2 m opening when wanted (privacy / cooling the bedroom alone).

- The curtain track will mount **behind the boxed-in steel lintel, on the bedroom side**, so the track is hidden from the living room.
- **Not in this Phase 02 quote** — spec'd separately when the time comes.
- Phase 02 must leave the bedroom-side back face of the lintel boxing accessible so the track can be added later without rework.

### Wall-hung shelves — later, separate scope

The **~3 m wall between the new bedroom and the bathroom** will be fitted with wall-hung shelves running its full length. These shelves are intended as **all the storage** for the new bedroom — replacing the wardrobe role.

- **System: HomePro.** HomePro stocks a modular wall-hung shelving system designed for exactly this use. Bought and installed via HomePro (or DIY off the same kit).
- **Not in this Phase 02 quote** — spec'd separately when the time comes (likely Joe + Mon DIY, after they've painted).
- This wall is **not** being demolished in Phase 02; it stays untouched, ready to receive shelves later.
- The wall is **masonry** (confirmed) — shelf fixings can be drilled and anchored anywhere, no Phase 02 prep work needed.
- No conduit, junction, or service-line work is needed inside this wall in Phase 02.

## Phase 02 scope is fixed — nothing else

The Phase 02 quote must cover **only** the items above. Do not add anything else
to the quote. In particular:

- **No painting.** Joe and Mon will paint themselves. No paint, paint prep, or
  painting labour in this Phase 02 quote.
- **No second AC unit.** The house's amperage cannot run two ACs at once. The
  plan here is a single AC, repositioned. A second AC is only viable if the
  household amperage is upgraded first — both are separate scopes.
- **No new ceiling lights.** The two existing ceiling-light fittings stay in place.
- **No ceiling work beyond the infill strip** where the leaf wall used to be.
- **No removal of the rest of the bedroom-to-living-room wall** — only the door-and-stub section comes out.
- **No bathroom / toilet tile work.** Separate Phase 02 line item — see [PHASES](AAA-PHASES.md).
- **No curtain.** Separate scope, later.
- **No wall-hung shelves.** Separate scope, later — see "Wall-hung shelves" above. The bedroom/bathroom wall stays untouched in Phase 02.

If the builder believes extra work is needed, raise it separately before
quoting — do not bundle it into this Phase 02 line item.
