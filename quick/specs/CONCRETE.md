## Steps

1. **Neutralise** - Mix 1kg baking soda into a large bucket of water. Scrub entire area, rinse 3 times. Leave surface damp (not dry, not wet puddles).
2. **Mix** - Half bag at a time (10kg + 4 litres water, ratio 2.5:1). Mix by hand with stick/paddle until no lumps. Let sit 5 minutes. Use within 1 hour. Pour into roller tray.
3. **Coat 1** - Surface must be damp but no wet sheen. Roll on with medium-nap roller.
4. **Coat 2** - Wait 30-90 minutes. Mix second batch if needed. Roll on perpendicular to coat 1. Coverage: 1-1.5 kg/m².
5. **Cure** - Protect from water minimum 3 days. Full cure 7-14 days.

---

## Notes

- **Safety:** Wear gloves, dust mask, eye protection.
- **Test first:** Buy the 1kg bag, try on a corner before committing to the 20kg bag.
- 1 hour pot life after mixing - don't mix the whole bag at once.
- Result: smooth, matte grey, waterproof surface.
