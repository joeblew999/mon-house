### Posting Tips

1. **Best time:** 7-9 AM or 6-9 PM Thai time
2. **Lead with exterior photo** - front of house first
3. **Include price in title** - gets more clicks
4. **Use Thai for local groups** - better reach
5. **Repost every 3-5 days** - groups move fast
